This is a demo site built with **Friendo** — your site is a folder, you build it
locally, and you publish it anywhere.

This post is a plain Markdown file at `content/blog/welcome-to-friendo.md`. When
you run `friendo serve`, it's compiled into the site database and rendered by the
template at `pages/blog/[slug].html`.

Scroll to the bottom of any post to try the **community features** — sign in with
just your email, leave a reaction, and post a comment.
