Since it keeps coming up: a sign on your own property may be up to six square feet
without a permit. Larger than that, you file form Z-4 with the clerk, which costs
nothing and takes about a week.

Lit signs are a separate matter and that is the part people are upset about. The
council takes it up again on the 14th. Open session, come and say your piece.
