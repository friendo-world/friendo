# friendo v0.8 — roadmap

> **Status: proposed 2026-10-20 as 0.7; renumbered 0.8 on 2026-10-27, when the visitor work shipped as v0.7. Open questions pending.** Scoped the day the 0.6 demo site
> was committed, from the shipped surface (the inbox, the Resend seam, event reminders, the
> visitor work of October) and the *Deferred* list at the end of
> [v0.6-roadmap.md](v0.6-roadmap.md). Builds on [v0.6-roadmap.md](v0.6-roadmap.md),
> [calendar-plan.md](calendar-plan.md) and the visitor interactions work (anonymous
> posting, drop boxes, visitor chat, RSVP reminders).
>
> Code references are files and function names as of 2026-10-20. Re-check them before
> building.

**Scope theme: the inbox reaches people who aren't on the site.** In 0.6 a member learns
what happened only by visiting. In 0.8 the same notes arrive by email: one summary a day
by default, sooner when a date is involved, with a way out in every message. Nothing new
is invented to say — email is a second way of delivering the inbox, not a second system.

**Why this and not direct messages, blocking or the desktop editor.** Every social feature
shipped in 0.6 is quieter than it should be: an invitation nobody sees, a join request
that waits until the moderator happens to visit. The 0.6 plan said twice that
notifications "need email to feel finished". Direct messages would add one more thing
that goes unseen until email exists. And the work turns up a bug worth fixing on its own
(see *Where things stand*, scheduling).

## Where things stand at v0.7

| Piece | Now |
|---|---|
| Inbox | `notifications` (migration `0017`): five kinds — `follow`, `comment`, `join_request`, `membership`, `event_invite` — written through `db.Notify` ([data/notifications.go](../runtime/go/data/notifications.go)), de-duplicated by (recipient, kind, target, actor), never about something you did yourself. Read by `<friendo-inbox>`, the badge on `<friendo-signin>`, `{{ user.unread }}`. No switch of its own: on while `follows` or `groups` is. Never syncs or exports. |
| Email | One seam, [email/email.go](../runtime/go/email/email.go): `Send(to, subject, text)` over Resend, plain text only, `Configured()` = `RESEND_API_KEY` + `FRIENDO_EMAIL_FROM`. Two things go out: sign-in codes and event reminders. The docs promise, in [social-graph.md](../docs/content/docs/social-graph.md): "Nothing is emailed. The sign-in code is the only email a friendo site sends." The reminder's footer promises "There's nothing else coming from this address." Both sentences change in 0.8. |
| Reminders | Offered to visitors only (`<friendo-rsvp>` hides the box for a signed-in member, though the API would accept it). `rsvps.reminder_email` / `reminder_link` / `reminder_sent` (migration `0026`); `db.SendDueReminders` runs from a 5-minute loop in [server/reminders.go](../runtime/go/server/reminders.go), started by `server.Start` — i.e. **by `friendo serve` only**. The network dispatcher calls `server.BuildSite` and never starts the loop, so **on friendo.world reminders are collected and never sent.** The link in a reminder is the event page's address *as the browser saw it*, because the server has no idea what its own public address is. |
| Addresses | A member's email is `users.email` (the sign-in identity). A visitor's reminder email lives on the RSVP row only. |
| Preferences | None per member. `users.auth_methods` is the precedent for a small per-account JSON column. |
| Scheduling | One goroutine per `friendo serve` process; nothing in network mode. The dispatcher's LRU closes a site's DB when its handler is evicted, so background work can't borrow the cached handle. `registry.Sites()` lists every site folder. |
| Site identity | `[site] name` and `timezone` (`LoadSiteLocation`). No `url`. On the network, `AccountAuth.siteURL` builds a subdomain's address from the request in hand (scheme, port); nothing builds one without a request, and nothing prefers a verified custom domain. |
| Rate limits | `rate_limits` (migration `0009`) throttles what members *do*. Nothing throttles what the site *sends*. |

## The decisions (proposed; confirm or overturn in *Answers*)

1. **Email delivers the inbox; it doesn't have its own list of things to say.** Every email a
   member gets is made of inbox notes, and anything that isn't a note isn't emailed. The
   writers (`db.Notify`) don't change; a sender reads notes that haven't been emailed yet.
   *Why:* one table, one word ("inbox"), and the 0.6 shape was built "so a digest can read
   it later". *Declined:* a separate `emails` queue; emailing things with no inbox note
   (a new chat message, a new post in a group).
2. **One summary a day, sooner when a date is involved.** A member gets at most one email a
   day — at 8:00 in the site's timezone — listing every note since the last one. Two kinds
   can't wait a day: an **event invitation** (`event_invite`) and a **join request** to a
   moderator (`join_request`). Those go out on the next tick, by themselves, and are then
   excluded from the summary. *Why:* the inbox's de-dupe already keeps a follow storm to one
   note, but one email per note is how a site becomes spam; and an invitation to Saturday
   that arrives Sunday is worse than none. *Declined:* an email per note for everyone; a
   per-member choice of speed (one more setting a novice has to understand).
3. **On by default, with the way out in every message.** Every member whose site can send
   mail gets the daily email unless they turn it off — in the inbox, in the admin, or by
   the unsubscribe link in any email (no sign-in needed; it's a signed link). Emails carry
   `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers so mail clients offer one-click
   unsubscribe. *Why:* an opt-in nobody is asked about is an opt-out that nobody finds; the
   OTP email already established the address as a channel. **This is the question most
   worth the user's call — see open question 1.** *Declined:* off until a member turns it
   on; on for new members only.
4. **One scheduler per process, visiting every site.** A `jobs` loop (`runtime/go/server`
   or a new `runtime/go/jobs`) ticks every 5 minutes; `friendo serve` gives it one site,
   `friendo network serve` gives it `registry.Sites()`. Per site it opens the DB, runs the
   due work (reminders, then inbox email), closes it. The existing `runReminders` becomes
   the first job. *Why:* this is the bug fix — reminders start going out on the network —
   and the digest needs the same loop. The dispatcher's cache is deliberately not involved:
   the job's handle is its own, short-lived, and SQLite in WAL mode is fine with two
   writers taking turns. *Knobs for later:* skip suspended sites; stagger sites across the
   tick; batch sends. Not needed at friendo.world's size.
5. **The site learns its own address.** An email needs links, and the server has never
   known its public URL. `[site] url = "https://…"` in `friendo.toml` is new; on the network
   it is filled in from the subdomain or verified custom domain, so tenants never set
   it. With no URL, an email still goes out but names things instead of
   linking them, and says where the site is by name. `reminder_link` keeps working as is
   (it was captured by the browser). *Declined:* remembering the origin of the last request
   (works until the site is pushed from a laptop).
6. **A site-level switch and a member-level choice, both two-valued.** The site:
   `[settings] inbox_email = true` (DB `notifications.email`, default on; shown in the
   admin's Settings with the provider's status next to it, greyed when no provider is set).
   The member: `email = "daily" | "never"`, default `daily`, a column on `users`
   (migration `0027`). *Why:* novices should see at most one switch each. *Declined:*
   per-kind settings (follow me / comment on me / …).

## Tier A — one scheduler, reminders on the network

- Move `runReminders` into a jobs loop that takes a site list. `friendo serve` passes its
  one site; the network passes `registry.Sites()` (skipping suspended sites). Each tick
  opens the DB (`data.Open`), runs every job, closes it. Errors are logged per site and
  never stop the loop.
- A reminder due during the gap (any RSVP with a `reminder_email`, an occurrence inside the
  24-hour window, and `reminder_sent = ''`) goes out on the first tick after deploy; one
  that's already in the past stays silent — `SendDueReminders` already does this.
- Test: the reminders test with a fake `send` gets a network-shaped sibling (two site
  folders, one tick, two sends). Ships on its own as **0.7.x** if the rest of 0.8 drags: it
  is a bug fix.

## Tier B — the daily email

- Migration `0027`: `notifications.emailed`, `users.email_inbox`, and the switch default.
- `db.SiteURL()` and `[site] url`; on the network the jobs loop passes each site its
  address (subdomain or verified domain, `https`), since a job has no request to read a
  scheme or port from.
- The `inbox_email` job, per site, when the provider is configured and the switch is on:
  1. **Prompt:** every un-emailed note of a prompt kind, grouped by recipient account
     (an account's inbox is the union over its personas — the existing rule), one email
     per account per tick, marked `emailed`.
  2. **Daily:** once per site-local day, after `dailyAt`: every account with any un-emailed
     note and `email_inbox = daily`, one email listing them, marked `emailed`. "Once per
     day" is a `site_settings` row (`notifications.email_last_daily = <date>`), not a
     per-member mark, so a restart at 8:03 doesn't send twice.
  - A member who read a note on the site before the daily email still gets it in the
    summary (read ≠ emailed). The de-dupe index means a note is never emailed twice.
- `email.SendInboxDaily` / `SendInboxPrompt` are the two templates; both end with the
  unsubscribe line. The reminder footer's "nothing else coming from this address" becomes
  "You asked for this reminder when you RSVP'd." (members get the daily email too; visitors
  only ever get the reminder).
- The unsubscribe route (`/_/unsubscribe`), the `List-Unsubscribe` headers (the Resend
  payload gains `headers`), and a test that the token round-trips and a wrong one is a 400.
- Sending is best-effort: a provider error logs and leaves `emailed` empty, so the next
  tick retries; a member whose address bounces keeps being tried (no webhooks in 0.8).

## Tier C — the choice, in-page and in the admin

- `GET/PUT /_/api/me/email`; `<friendo-inbox>` shows "Email me a daily summary — on / off"
  under the list when the site can send (`email: "off"` hides it). The persona switcher
  and `<friendo-profile>` are untouched: this is about the account, not a profile.
- Admin: Settings → Email (status, switch, test send); People shows the column.
- `{{ user.email_inbox }}` in the context, for a site that wants its own control.
- **Members get "Remind me" on an RSVP.** `<friendo-rsvp>` shows the reminder box only to
  visitors today (`!user && visitorMay`); the API already accepts `email` from a member and
  `rsvpPayload` already returns their `reminder_email`. For a signed-in member it becomes a
  checkbox, "Email me the day before", that sends their account address — no typing. The
  memory note from 2026-10-08 named this the natural follow-up.

## Deferred (named so the next release starts from here)

- **Replies and mentions** — a note when someone replies to your comment or writes `@pat`;
  new inbox kinds, so they'd email for free. Not in 0.8 because they need the comment
  threading decision.
- **Email for chats and group posts** — decision 1 keeps email to inbox notes; "new post
  in a group you're in" would be a new note kind first.
- **Bounces and complaints** — Resend webhooks turning a bad address to `never`.
- **Direct messages**, **blocking / muting**, **slots** — still deferred from 0.6.
- **Per-kind choices** — declined in decision 6; revisit if members ask.

## Open questions

1. **On by default for existing members?** Decision 3 says yes with a one-click way out. The
   alternative is on for members who join after the upgrade and off for everyone already
   there. Every existing friendo.world member was told "nothing is emailed".
2. **Prompt kinds.** Is `join_request` to a moderator really a prompt, or does it belong in
   the daily email with a `membership` approval? (Invitations are the clear case.)
3. **The hour.** 8:00 site-local for everyone, or a site setting (`[settings] email_hour`)?
   One more setting, but a church and a bar want different mornings.
4. **`[site] url` on `friendo serve`.** Required for links, or should the server also accept
   a `--url` flag the way it takes `--port`? A self-hoster behind Caddy has one address and
   two places to write it.
5. **Where the member's choice lives.** Decision 6 puts it in `<friendo-inbox>`'s footer. The
   other candidates are `<friendo-signin>`'s menu (where the persona switcher is) and a new
   `pages/account.html`. The inbox is the only one that doesn't invent a page.
6. **Ship Tier A alone first as 0.7.1?** It fixes reminders on friendo.world today and
   has no decisions in it.
